Escape the Desk

You're Timothy the pencil. Get off either end of the desk, and don't let a hand grab you.

or press Enter

← → Walk, or roll when you're lying down. Rolling keeps its speed.
↑ Jump, or stand back up.
↓ Tip over onto your side. You're low enough to duck a high swipe.
Space Stab a hand with your tip. It jerks away, but you get duller and slower.
Sharpener: stand at it and press ↓. Makes you sharp again. If you're already sharp, you just get shorter.
R Restart · M Sound on or off
Watch for the warnings: a shadow means a slam or a guard, dots mean a dive, and a red ! at the side means a swipe.
